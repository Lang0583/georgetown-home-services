# Editorial voice — Georgetown Home Services

Locked house voice for all user-facing prose.

## Voice

**Third-person directory.** The site describes trades, companies, and local conditions. It does not coach the reader as “you,” sell urgency, or pretend to be a contractor.

- Publisher: Georgetown Home Services
- Byline: Matt (homeowner / editor), never a fake crew of experts
- Stance: comparison and education — not dispatch, not lead brokerage

## Do

- State facts: neighborhoods, clay soil, plant hardness bands, license boards, planning price bands
- Prefer short declarative sentences
- Name what the page is: a shortlist, a planning band, a city-sourced hardness table
- Keep second person only for literal UI instructions (“Enter a street address”) or legal/consent copy
- Say when something is unknown or unverified

## Do not

- Fake empathy openers (“If you own a home… you don’t think about plumbers until…”)
- Throat-clearing (“In today’s world,” “Whether you’re looking for,” “It’s important to note”)
- Brochure CTAs (“When you’re ready, compare… and contact providers directly”)
- Parallel listicle cadence repeated across every trade hub
- “This guide walks you through…” / “Use these pages to…” as the default lede
- “We help homeowners…” / “Georgetown Home Services helps…”
- Invented rankings, sold placement language, or insurance-scare urgency

## Page-type defaults

| Surface | Lead with |
|---------|-----------|
| Trade hub | What the trade covers locally + license fact + link to Best Of |
| Best Of | Inclusion criteria + how the shortlist is built + local risk factors |
| Cost guide | Planning band range + what moves the number |
| Water | City-published plant bands; no sink-test scare copy |
| Homepage | What the directory is / is not |

## Example (before → after)

**Before:** “If you own a home in Georgetown, TX, you do not think about plumbers when everything is working…”

**After:** “This directory lists Georgetown-area plumbers that meet published inclusion criteria. Placement is not sold. License numbers shown on cards were checked against TSBPE public records where available.”
